The reference artefact for anyone who wants to make a quantitative claim about formalised
regulation without needing employer clearance to publish it. 962 constitutive statements
carrying RuleML rules, built over the PrOnto privacy ontology by legal scholars, published
in the JURIX/AI-and-Law community.

**Why I keep coming back to it.** It is legal content formalised for legal purposes by
people with no stake in whatever I am arguing. That independence is the entire value: when
I measure something on my own insurance rule base I am measuring my own construction
choices, and a reviewer is right to say so.

**What I measured on it.** Constraint density — the fraction of a rule's antecedent
predicates that are actually discriminating rather than ubiquitous. Mean 0.462, 43
density-0 rules. Notes in
[Coverage is not constraint](/writing/coverage-is-not-constraint).

**What I still need from it.** The repository licence, confirmed in writing, before
redistributing the XML copy alongside a paper. Measuring it is fine either way;
redistribution is the part that needs checking.
